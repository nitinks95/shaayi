Welcome to Shaayi

Terms and conditions

Shaayi is an early preview. It is provided as is, without warranty. It reads your trackpad, and optionally a paired phone on your local network, only to draw on its canvas. Nothing you draw or any input data leaves your computer.

Permissions checklist

  • macOS: no extra permission is needed for the input paths that ship today.
  • Windows: no extra permission is needed for the input paths that ship today.

If a future input source needs a permission, this page will say so and link to the relevant system settings then.
